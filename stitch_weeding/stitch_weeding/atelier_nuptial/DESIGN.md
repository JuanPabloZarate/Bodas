## Brand & Style
The design system embodies bespoke luxury, timeless romance, and editorial grace. Designed for celebratory wedding platforms, bespoke digital invitations, and bridal stationery suites, the aesthetic rejects the plastic, utilitarian feel of standard SaaS in favor of fine art printmaking traditions: blind letterpress embossing, delicate deckled margins, and poetic pacing.

The experience evokes intimacy, quiet luxury, and emotional warmth. The style is rooted in **Editorial Minimalism and Tactile Warmth**—generous breathing room, deliberate asymmetrical balance, refined ornamental borders, hairline dividers, and classical typography pairings reminiscent of high-end monographs and artisanal paper goods.

## Colors
The palette evokes botanical estates, artisanal linen stationery, and warm candlelight. 

- **Primary (`#6B7F6D`)**: Romantic eucalyptus sage. Grounded, organic, and poetic. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key botanical vectors, and refined highlights.
- **Secondary (`#C9937E`)**: Muted warm terracotta rose. Radiates warmth and tactile luxury; ideal for gentle focus indicators, badge backgrounds, and secondary focal elements.
- **Tertiary (`#B47B65`)**: Rose bronze accent. Applied sparingly for hairline rule dividers, embossed borders, monogram accents, and gilded flourishes.
- **Neutral (`#2C2523`)**: Deep espresso bronze. Replaces harsh synthetic blacks to preserve warm intimacy while ensuring crisp typographic legibility.

### Surface Tiers & Canvas Tones
- **Canvas Base**: `#FAF8F5` (Champagne Ivory), evoking handmade cotton rag paper.
- **Surface Alt / Container**: `#F5EFEB` (Milled Warm Alabaster) for nested modular cards and elevated RSVP containers.
- **Border / Subtle Accents**: `#E8DED1` (Warm Blush Sand) used for hairline structural strokes, framing rings, and envelope trim metaphors.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on high-contrast drama between the stately, high-contrast strokes of Playfair Display and the delicate, geometric airiness of light-weight Montserrat. 

Display and headline copy should frequently employ natural title-casing and occasional italic flourishes for vows, couple names, and poetic introductions. Section subheaders, metadata tags, and formal dates are set in `label-uppercase` with generous letter spacing to evoke traditional letterpress typesetting. Body copy preserves elevated line heights to maintain a breathable reading cadence.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a centered, editorial grid system prioritizing white space as a first-class aesthetic element.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column responsive grid on desktop with a maximum content canvas of 1180px for standard flows, narrowing to an intimate 760px column for wedding details and RSVP experiences.
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Spacing uses deliberate vertical intervals. Breakpoints correspond to mobile (<640px), tablet (640px–1024px), and desktop (>1024px). Section separators rely heavily on wide vertical gaps (`space-xl` scaled 1.5x to 2x) rather than dense, heavy structural boxes.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids harsh drop shadows and heavy synthetic dark overlays. Instead, elevation mirrors physical layering of luxury paper stocks, deckled invites, and vellum overlays.

1. **Layered Paper Tones**: Elevation is primarily established by placing lighter cards (`#FAF8F5`) upon slightly deeper parchment grounds (`#F5EFEB`).
2. **Candlelit Ambient Shadows**: Floating elements (such as RSVP modals or celebration itineraries) utilize an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with the deep espresso bronze: `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(44, 37, 35, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 37, 35, 0.03)`.
3. **Double & Hairline Borders**: Cards and framed modules feature subtle 1px inner or outer strokes in `#E8DED1` or `#B47B65` with a 20% alpha, resembling gilt-edged cardstock.
4. **Vellum Frosted Panels**: Navigation ribbons and floating dialogs utilize high-transparency ivory backdrop filters (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to mimic semi-sheer tissue wraps.

## Shapes
The shape language takes inspiration from bespoke fine-art stationery: mostly crisp, architectural silhouettes complemented by gentle, softly softened corners (`roundedness: 1`, 0.25rem / 4px base radius) to ensure cards feel tangible rather than sharp and digital. 

Select featured visual media (such as couple portrait frames and floral hero showcases) may use classical architectural arches (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 4px 4px`) to create an authentic cathedral or botanical conservatory silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (RSVP / Accept)**: Filled in Sage Green (`#6B7F6D`) with Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) typography, set in uppercase with tracking. Padding: `12px 28px`. Hover: shifts to deep sage bronze tint with a smooth 200ms transition.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Calendar / Registry)**: Clean outline button with a 1px border in `#C9937E`, text in `#2C2523`, transparent background. On hover, fills with 8% terracotta tint.
- **Text Link**: Neutral text with a persistent hairline underline offset by 4px in `#B47B65`.

### Cards & Stationery Enclosures
- Contained within `#FAF8F5` surface with a 1px border in `#E8DED1`.
- Featured ceremonial cards can adopt an inset double-border motif (a 1px outer stroke, a 4px blank gap, and a delicate inner border in `#C9937E` at 30% opacity).
- Card padding remains generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Clean underline or refined framed inputs with a warm alabaster background (`#F5EFEB`).
- 1px border in `#E8DED1`, transitioning to `#6B7F6D` on focus with no heavy outer glow ring.
- Floating placeholder labels styled in `Montserrat` light, elevating smoothly into uppercase metadata labels upon focus.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular indicators with a 1px `#C9937E` rim. Checked states display a delicate inner dot in `#6B7F6D` or a botanical leaf glyph.

### Dividers & Monograms
- Decorative section breaks featuring a hairline horizontal divider in `#E8DED1` interrupted at the center by a subtle diamond, botanical flourish, or centered serif Roman numeral.

### RSVP Badges & Chips
- Pill or gently softened chips with an ivory ground, 1px border in `#C9937E`, and compact uppercase typography (`label-uppercase`), used for dietary preference tags, schedule times, and dress code advisories.
